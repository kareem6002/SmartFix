# SmartFix 🛠️

### Smart Home Maintenance & Technician Booking Platform

**SmartFix** is a home-maintenance platform that connects customers with trusted technicians to solve everyday home repair and maintenance problems. The platform helps users find the right technician, submit service requests, book appointments, and track their repairs.

## 📌 Project Overview

Finding a reliable technician for home repairs can be difficult and time-consuming. Customers may struggle to find the right professional, understand which service they need, or keep track of their appointments.

SmartFix aims to make this process easier by providing a single platform where customers can request home maintenance services and connect with suitable technicians.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

People often face challenges when looking for home maintenance services, including:

- Difficulty finding reliable and qualified technicians.
- Uncertainty about which type of technician is needed.
- Time-consuming communication and appointment scheduling.
- Lack of a convenient way to track service requests and evaluate completed work.

## 💡 Our Solution

SmartFix provides a centralized platform where customers can describe their home maintenance problems, upload photos, receive a preliminary problem category and recommended technician type, book appointments, and follow the progress of their requests.

The platform also gives technicians a way to manage service requests and allows administrators to manage the platform.

## ✨ Main Features

### 👤 Customer Features
- Create an account and manage a personal profile.
- Browse available home maintenance services.
- Submit a repair request and describe the problem.
- Upload photos to help identify the general problem category.
- Receive a preliminary category and a recommended technician type.
- Browse technician profiles, skills, experience, ratings, and availability.
- Book appointments and track service request status.
- Rate and review technicians after service completion.

### 🔧 Technician Features
- Create and manage a technician profile.
- Specify skills, experience, and availability.
- View incoming service requests.
- Accept or manage appointments.
- Update the status of ongoing and completed jobs.
- View ratings and manage work-related information.

### 🛡️ Admin Features
- Manage customer and technician accounts.
- Review and manage service requests and bookings.
- Handle complaints and monitor platform activity.
- View basic platform statistics and analytics.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. The customer creates an account and selects a home maintenance service.
2. The customer describes the problem and may upload a photo.
3. SmartFix provides a preliminary problem category and suggests a suitable technician type.
4. The customer browses suitable technicians and selects an available appointment.
5. The technician receives and manages the service request.
6. The customer tracks the request until the service is completed.
7. The customer can leave a rating and review.
