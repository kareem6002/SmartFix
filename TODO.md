# SmartFix update outcomes

- [x] Technicians, engineers, and companies can add previous work with a service label, description, and photos.
- [x] Technicians can apply to engineers from their SmartFix profile.
- [x] Engineers can apply to companies from their SmartFix profile.
- [x] Engineers can send hiring offers to technicians.
- [x] Companies can send hiring offers to engineers.
- [x] Recipients receive applications and offers in an Inbox tab and can accept or decline them.
- [x] Accepted collaboration updates the recipient's SmartFix work affiliation.

- [x] Customers can explore technician profiles and book technicians for simple fixes; they can explore and book engineers for complex repairs that need coordinated technician teams; and they can explore and book companies for design/build work or property checks while abroad. All provider listings use larger, character-photo cards.
- [x] The technician navbar has clearer, distinct destinations for jobs, inbox, work history, and applications.
- [x] Email invitations have been removed from the engineer's My Team tab. Team members appear in large cards with Explore and Remove actions; applications and offers remain in their dedicated area.
- [x] Engineers can view a company's portfolio when reviewing an offer from that company.
- [x] Email invitations have been removed from the company's My Team tab. Engineers appear in large cards with Explore and Remove actions; applications and offers remain in their dedicated area.
- [x] The company inbox contains only customer project requests and engineer applications.
- [x] Light and dark themes are available and persisted across sessions, including the welcome tablet.
- [x] Visible interface copy for the updated flows is translated in English and Arabic, with RTL support and matching translation keys.
